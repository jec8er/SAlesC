# ORBIT U.S. policy-readiness pack

## Scope

ORBIT supports one launch jurisdiction per mission across all 50 U.S. states
and Washington, D.C. The pack is an operational checklist and approval gate. It
is not legal advice, a substitute for counsel, or a guarantee that a business
complies with every federal, state, or local rule.

Every new mission records:

- state code and jurisdiction name;
- activity classification;
- whether personal data is sold or shared;
- required documents and operational controls;
- specialized-review blockers; and
- an evidence-backed owner approval event.

Legacy missions without this metadata remain launch-blocked.

## Required baseline documents

1. Privacy notice.
2. Terms of service.
3. Acceptable-use policy.
4. AI disclosure.
5. Refund and cancellation policy.
6. Data-retention and security plan.
7. State privacy addendum.
8. Automated-decision disclosure when ORBIT materially automates a decision.
9. Sale/sharing opt-out notice when personal data is sold or shared.
10. Activity-specific financial or child-privacy disclosures when applicable.

These are document requirements, not pre-approved legal text. The final policy
artifact must link evidence of the state-specific review before it can be
approved.

## Required operational controls

- Maintain a data inventory and stated purposes.
- Publish a privacy-request channel.
- Record consent and policy versions.
- Substantiate advertising and AI claims.
- Assign an incident-response owner.
- Provide human review and an appeal path for automated decisions.
- Honor applicable opt-outs and preference signals when data is sold/shared.
- Obtain state-specific legal review before launch.

## Hard blockers

ORBIT will not approve the policy task when a mission is classified as:

- `children-directed`, until COPPA parental-consent and counsel review is
  resolved outside ORBIT; or
- `investment-advice`, until counsel determines the registration and advisory
  obligations.

Financial education receives a disclaimer requirement and a control prohibiting
personalized investment advice. Paper-mode analytics do not become live trading
or personalized advice through this feature.
