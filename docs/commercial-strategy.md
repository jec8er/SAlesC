# Initial sales motion — unvalidated offer

## Evidence and positioning

The source repositories contained a food-ordering HTML prototype in a README, a minimal Express template, and an empty audio repository. There are no evidenced customers, contracts, processor collections or demand metrics.

WhatsApp already supports catalog/cart conversations, and Square already provides online ordering. The business hypothesis is therefore paid **implementation, content and handoff**, not charging solely for a basic catalog feature.

Primary sources checked October 11, 2026:

- https://faq.whatsapp.com/5495780857144067/
- https://squareup.com/help/us/en/article/8566-set-up-an-online-ordering-profile

## Offer to test

Direct Commerce Launch: one business catalog, up to 10 approved products, direct WhatsApp request flow, three reviewed launch scripts, one recorded voiceover and one owner handoff. Proposed setup: USD 199. Proposed optional update retainer: USD 39/month with a defined workload limit and no charge without agreement. Adjust after actual discovery and paid-delivery time measurement.

Buyer hypothesis: an independent merchant with real products, a working fulfillment process, an owner decision-maker and a demonstrable ordering or presentation problem. Unknown willingness to pay. No named prospects are asserted.

## Execution

1. Juan selects one reachable business and confirms permission to use its product information. No unsolicited outreach has been sent.
2. Ask the owner how orders arrive, which requests fail, who updates prices, what fulfillment they support and who approves the purchase.
3. Build a bounded sample from approved facts, demonstrate the exported catalog and test the destination phone number with the owner.
4. Agree written scope, price, revision limit, due date and payment method before the full delivery.
5. Deliver the complete files, check one customer request, collect and document the actual payment. Record labor, fees and any refund.
6. Offer recurring updates only when a recurring need is observed. Measure paid conversion and margin before broadening acquisition.

Suggested owner: Juan for customer contact, approvals and collections; app for record keeping and export. Dates: TBD until a real buyer is selected. The main risks are unvalidated willingness to pay, weak differentiation, excessive delivery labor and unconfigured collection methods. The next evidence required is one owner discovery and a paid scope, not a traffic projection.

## Unit economics

At 199 gross, 40 delivery/labor cost and 6 processing fee, contribution is 153 before tax. This is an illustrative scenario, not actual performance or a processor fee quote. Five setups produce 995 gross only if five customers actually pay. Track collections separately from proposal value.

## Launch boundary

The app is owner-private. Public buyer access, payment processing and automatic external publishing remain separate integrations. Never describe the manual ledger as independently verified financial reporting, microphone recording as AI voice generation, or template scripts as autonomous agents.
